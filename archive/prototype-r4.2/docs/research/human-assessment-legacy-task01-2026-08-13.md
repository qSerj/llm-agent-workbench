## Rubric Feedback

The reviewer found the meanings of some dimensions insufficiently clear and a
universal 0–2 scale too coarse for complex work, while even a 0–10 scale would
create false precision for a nearly binary task this small. For simple tasks,
explicit checks and `PASS`/`FAIL` may be enough. Richer tasks need
task-specific dimensions and anchors rather than merely more points.

The reviewer also proposed an iterative path for this class of defect:
generate the result, have a small model verify its factual claims, and revise
the result from the findings. This becomes a concrete hypothesis for the
future `solver → reviewer → fixer` experiments.

## Consequence

Scores and verdict answer different questions. A verdict records fitness for
the task's acceptance policy; dimension scores describe quality and defects.
The workbench must preserve both without deriving one from an undocumented
sum or universal threshold.
